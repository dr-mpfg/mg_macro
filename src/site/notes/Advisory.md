# Work with me

> Macro-financial strategy for institutions that need to know how a system clears before they need to know where it trades.

<br />

I take commissioned work from two directions. Ship finance and freight: lenders, lessors, owners, marine insurers and platforms. Allocators and credit funds: where the question is euro-area sovereign and monetary risk read structurally rather than off the spread. The brief is usually a version of the same one -- below the headline, what is the actual structure of claims here, where does the pressure sit, and what breaks first.

# Areas

## Shipping and trade finance

Freight exposure is working-capital exposure. Chartering and freight-rate risk, tonne-mile and throughput demand, forwarder and carrier working capital, trade finance structures, and the capital stack behind a fleet -- including Greek and Mediterranean maritime economics. From inside the industry, and written up in [[Cargo & Collateral\|Cargo & Collateral]].

## Sovereign credit and monetary structure

Read at the level of the mechanism, not the spread. Sovereign risk and debt management, monetary transmission, collateral and repo, fragmentation and the euro-area settlement plumbing beneath it. Where the structural view comes from: [[Research Projects/Eurozone\|Eurozone]], [[Research Projects/Subordination\|Subordination]], [[Research Projects/Macro-Structures & DMO\|Macro-Structures & DMO]].

## Balance-sheet modelling

For questions that need a structure rather than a forecast. Bespoke modelling of a monetary architecture, a sector or a firm as interlocking balance sheets, built to be interrogated rather than believed. Method: [[Research Projects/Balance-Sheet Modelling\|Balance-Sheet Modelling]].
