> *Macro-financial strategist -- shipping and trade finance, monetary architecture, and the credit behind physical flows*

__[Contact](mailto:mg.macroeconomics@gmail.com) ~ [Cargo & Collateral](https://cargoandcollateral.substack.com/) ~ [CV](https://app.box.com/s/18pq9r7919gcxvu23hk5v4wda4hq6dq4) ~ [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/matteogiordano-economist/) ~ [ORCiD](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-1588-6735)__

<br />

I read financial systems as claims and obligations: who owes what to whom, on whose balance sheet, funded how. The method came out of the euro area -- repo, collateral, TARGET, sovereign debt -- and it does not stay there. A rearmament programme is a working-capital cycle before it is a capability. Freight is a credit business wearing logistics clothes, and the credit is what breaks first.

I am a macro strategist and financial analyst in the shipping industry, based in Greece, and an Associate Researcher at the Global Climate Forum in Berlin, where I build balance-sheet models for the [OBFA-TRANSFORM Project](https://obfa-transform.eu) and the [Monetary Architecture Online Tool](https://monetary-architecture.com/). I hold a PhD in economics from SOAS University of London, where I wrote on monetary subordination in the Eurozone under Prof. Costas Lapavitsas and lectured until 2025. Until September 2026 I was a defence economist at the [UKDSC](https://www.ukdsc.org), within the [Ministry of Defence](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/ministry-of-defence) ecosystem, where my reports contributed to shaping MoD policy on measuring defence-industrial productivity, evaluating programme viability, assessing supply-chain resilience and vulnerability, and defining industrial readiness. I am a dual Italian-British national.

# Three lines of work

## 1 · Shipping and trade finance

> Chartering and freight rates, port throughput and tonne-mile demand, forwarder and carrier working capital, and the credit chain behind a single shipment -- read from inside the industry rather than from a screen. Published weekly in [[Cargo & Collateral\|Cargo & Collateral]].

## 2 · Macro-financial strategy and research

> Commissioned structural reads for allocators, lenders, insurers and policy institutions: where the pressure sits in a sovereign, a sector or a market structure, and what it takes to shift it. What I take on, and how: [[Advisory\|Work with me]].

## 3 · Monetary architecture and hierarchy

> The long-running research programme -- collateral, repo, TARGET and subordination in the euro area, mapped as interlocking balance sheets -- with the Global Climate Forum's OBFA-TRANSFORM project and its public [Monetary Architecture tool](https://monetary-architecture.com/). The projects: [[Research Projects/Research Projects\|Research Projects]]. Repo, debt management and central-bank balance sheets also run through [[Cargo & Collateral\|Cargo & Collateral]].
