# Macro-financial strategy and research

## [[Research Projects/Macro-Structures & DMO\|Macro-Structure, DMOs and Monetary Regimes]]
With Enrico Pulieri, in preparation. The side of central banking the independence literature leaves out: debt management offices, their claimed independence, and their synergies and tensions with the central bank.

## [[Research Projects/Defence & Security Economics\|Modelling the defence-industrial base]]
Applied work, 2025-2026. Input-Output models of inter-sectoral linkage and supply-chain dependency, gravity models of defence trade and burden-sharing, and the fiscal sustainability of sustained rearmament.

# Monetary architecture and hierarchy

## [[Research Projects/Balance-Sheet Modelling\|Balance-Sheet Modelling & Monetary Architecture]]
Ongoing, with the Global Climate Forum. The methodological core: money as a hierarchy of interlocking balance sheets, crises as the moment they stop clearing. Output includes the public [Monetary Architecture Online Tool](https://monetary-architecture.com/) and long-run financial-flow analysis from 1870.

## [[Research Projects/Eurozone\|Asymmetries, Imbalances and Fragmentation in the Eurozone]]
Ongoing. Why the euro area's imbalances sit in its infrastructure -- CCPs, CSDs, the ECB and the NCBs -- and how monetary integration drove the financialisation of member economies. Three empirical pillars, built on official datasets that are largely unused.

## [[Research Projects/Subordination\|Monetary Subordination in the Eurozone]]
PhD thesis, SOAS, 2025. Monetary hierarchy, hybrid money and selective fragmentation as the three pillars of subordination inside a monetary union, with Italy as the historical case and a six-country comparison of subordinate financialisation.

## [[Research Projects/GEGI Study\|Transformation of the Eurozone Architecture]]
Published 2025, Boston University GDP Center. Structured process-tracing plus balance-sheet visualisation applied to the Eurocrisis, arguing that contagion across euro-area and US balance sheets was the endogenous force that reshaped the EMU.
