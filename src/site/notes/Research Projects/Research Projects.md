# Macro-financial strategy and research

## [[Research Projects/Macro-Structures & DMO\|Macro-Structure, DMOs and Monetary Regimes]]

Central bank independence is half the story. Sovereign debt is run by a separate institution with its own claim to independence, and the two postures interact -- the side the CBI literature leaves out. With Enrico Pulieri, in preparation.

## [[Research Projects/Defence & Security Economics\|Modelling the defence-industrial base]]

Capability is downstream of industrial structure and financing. Input-Output models of inter-sectoral linkage and supply-chain dependency, gravity models of defence trade and burden-sharing, and the fiscal sustainability of sustained rearmament. Applied work, 2025-2026.

# Monetary architecture and hierarchy

## [[Research Projects/Balance-Sheet Modelling\|Balance-Sheet Modelling & Monetary Architecture]]

Money as a hierarchy of interlocking balance sheets, crises as the moment they stop clearing. The methodological core of everything else here. Outputs: the public [Monetary Architecture Online Tool](https://monetary-architecture.com/) and financial-flow analysis running from 1870. Ongoing, with the Global Climate Forum.

## [[Research Projects/Eurozone\|Asymmetries, Imbalances and Fragmentation in the Eurozone]]

The euro area's imbalances sit in its infrastructure -- CCPs, CSDs, the ECB and the NCBs -- and monetary integration is what financialised the member economies. Three empirical pillars, on official datasets that are largely unused. Ongoing.

## [[Research Projects/Subordination\|Monetary Subordination in the Eurozone]]

Monetary hierarchy, hybrid money and selective fragmentation as the three pillars of subordination inside a monetary union. Italy as the historical case; a six-country comparison of subordinate financialisation. PhD thesis, SOAS, 2025.

## [[Research Projects/GEGI Study\|Transformation of the Eurozone Architecture]]

Contagion across euro-area and US balance sheets was the endogenous force that reshaped the EMU -- not sovereign profligacy. Structured process-tracing plus balance-sheet visualisation, from the Global Financial Crisis to the 2014 asset purchase programmes. Published 2025, Boston University GDP Center.
