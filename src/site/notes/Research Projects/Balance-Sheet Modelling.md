# Balance-Sheet Modelling & Monetary Architecture

> Reading monetary systems through the interlocking balance sheets that constitute them — and turning that reading into practitioner-facing tools.

<br />

Much of my research rests on a single methodological conviction: that money and finance are best understood as a hierarchy of interlocking balance sheets, and that crises are what happen when those balance sheets can no longer clear. Balance-sheet modelling makes that structure visible, traces how elasticity and stress propagate through it, and supports both historical analysis and forward-looking scenarios.

<br />
<br />

# Strands

## 1 · The Monetary Architecture framework

> As Associate Researcher at the Global Climate Forum, I contribute to the [OBFA-TRANSFORM Project](https://obfa-transform.eu) and helped build the [Monetary Architecture Online Tool](https://monetary-architecture.com/) — a publicly accessible resource that renders the Eurozone's monetary architecture as navigable, practitioner-facing balance-sheet analysis. See also the [[Research Projects/GEGI Study\|GEGI Study]].

## 2 · Long-run financial-flow dynamics

> Quantitative analysis of trade, capital, and financial-flow dynamics over the very long run (1870–present), identifying structural breaks and regime shifts in globalisation and financial integration, and linking historical patterns to scenarios for financing large-scale transformations.

## 3 · Crisis dynamics through balance sheets

> A methodology combining structured process-tracing with balance-sheet visualisation to explain crisis-driven institutional change -- applied to the transformation of the Eurozone architecture from the Global Financial Crisis onward. See the [[Research Projects/GEGI Study\|GEGI Study]].

<br />
<br />

# Method

Balance-sheet mapping and visualisation, stock-flow-consistent accounting, long-run time-series analysis, and scenario frameworks — using primary and secondary data across private and public sectors.
