# Balance-Sheet Modelling & Monetary Architecture

Money is a hierarchy of interlocking balance sheets, and a crisis is the moment they stop clearing. That conviction is the methodological core of most of my research. Balance-sheet modelling makes the structure visible, traces how elasticity and stress propagate through it, and carries both historical analysis and forward scenarios.

<br />
<br />

# Strands

## 1 · The Monetary Architecture framework

As Associate Researcher at the Global Climate Forum I contribute to the [OBFA-TRANSFORM Project](https://obfa-transform.eu) and helped build the [Monetary Architecture Online Tool](https://monetary-architecture.com/) -- the euro area's architecture rendered as navigable, practitioner-facing balance-sheet analysis. See also the [[Research Projects/GEGI Study\|GEGI Study]].

## 2 · Long-run financial-flow dynamics

Trade, capital and financial flows from 1870 to the present, read for structural breaks and regime shifts in globalisation and financial integration, and linked to scenarios for financing large-scale transformation.

## 3 · Crisis dynamics through balance sheets

Structured process-tracing combined with balance-sheet visualisation, used to explain crisis-driven institutional change -- applied to the transformation of the euro-area architecture from the Global Financial Crisis onward. See the [[Research Projects/GEGI Study\|GEGI Study]].

<br />
<br />

# Method

Balance-sheet mapping and visualisation, stock-flow-consistent accounting, long-run time-series analysis, and scenario frameworks, on primary and secondary data across private and public sectors.
