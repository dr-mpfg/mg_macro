# Asymmetries, Imbalances, and Fragmentation in the Eurozone

![T2_EoP_plot.png](/img/user/Attachments/T2_EoP_plot.png)

The euro area's imbalances are built into its infrastructure. This project traces them to the plumbing -- CCPs, CSDs, the ECB and the national central banks -- and to the link between monetary integration and the financialisation of the member economies. Three empirical pillars, built on official datasets that are largely unused.

<br />
<br />

# Three pillars

## 1 · Monetary hierarchies in the EMU

Jurisdictions are tiered and legacy euros persist. TARGET is the backstop that holds the tiering together.

![Liabilities_nonEA_Euro_Plot.png](/img/user/Attachments/Liabilities_nonEA_Euro_Plot.png)

## 2 · Fragmentation and selective integration

Money markets fragment even with liquidity in excess. The shortage that binds is collateral.

![Origin_Collateral_plot.png](/img/user/Attachments/Origin_Collateral_plot.png)

## 3 · Capital and liquidity in hybrid refinancing

Where TARGET balances actually come from, and how the hybrid working of the mechanism produces them.
